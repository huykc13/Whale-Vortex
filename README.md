# Whale-Vortex
We are Whale Vortex, a diverse team of young innovators aged 12–19, united by curiosity, creativity, and a passion for making a difference. Like a whale transforming through the ocean's currents, we believe age is never a barrier to innovation. Together, we learn, create, and explore beyond limits!
